## 🏋️ 5-Day Workout Blueprint Overview
- **Day 1 (Mon) — Upper Push**: Upper Chest (Clavicular Head), Flat Barbell Bench, Seated DB Lateral Raises, Seated Barbell OHP, Overhead DB Extension, Cable Pushdowns.
- **Day 2 (Tue) — Athletic Lower A**: Smith Squat (Feet 4" Forward for pure quad drive & zero spinal shear), Bulgarian Split Squats, Leg Press (Narrow & Low for quad sweep), Standing Calf Raise (2s dead pause), Captain's Chair Leg Raises (APT reset).
- **Day 3 (Wed) — Upper Pull + Chicken Day**: Assisted Pull-Ups (Front only), Seated V-Bar Cable Row, Reverse Pec Deck Fly, DB Pullovers, Hammer Curls, Preacher Curls.
- **Day 4 (Thu) — Athletic Lower B**: Barbell Romanian Deadlifts (RDL), High & Wide Stance Leg Press (Gluteus Maximus isolation), Lying Leg Curls, Seated Calf Raises (Soleus anti-cramp engine), Forearm Plate Pinch Hold. *(48h buffer before Saturday cricket).*
- **Day 5 (Fri) — Upper Proportions**: Incline Chest Press Touch-up, Lateral Raise Drop-set finisher, Reverse Pec Deck, Close-Grip Bench Press, Cable Bicep Curls, Wrist Curls.
- **Day 6 (Sat) — 2-Hour Turf Cricket**: High-intensity fielding & sprint stamina (+50g clean lunch carbs + 1.5L hydration).
- **Day 7 (Sun) — Full Rest & Active Recovery**: 8k steps, mobility, and Chicken Day feast.
