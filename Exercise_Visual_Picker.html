Day 1: Upper Push (6 Dedicated Exercises)

Upper chest clavicular shelf, lateral delt width, and horseshoe triceps. Total: 19 working sets in ~65 mins.

Slot 1 • Clavicular Upper Chest

Incline Press (30° Angle)

3 sets × 8–10 reps
⚠️ Cue: Keep bench at strictly 30° (2nd or 3rd notch). Tuck elbows 45° relative to ribs. Press up and in.
Slot 2 • Mid/Lower Sternal Chest

Horizontal Press Pattern

3 sets × 8–10 reps
⚠️ Cue: Retract shoulder blades, arch lower back naturally. Lower bar to lower sternum.
Slot 3 • Lateral Deltoids (Shoulder Width)

Side Delt Abduction

4 sets × 12–15 reps
⚠️ Cue: Raise in the Scapular Plane (30° forward, not straight sideways). Lead with elbows!
Slot 4 • Anterior Deltoids

Overhead Shoulder Press

3 sets × 8–10 reps
⚠️ Cue: Brace core tight, press directly overhead without hyperextending lower back.
Slot 5 • Triceps (Long Head Thickness)

Overhead Extension Pattern

3 sets × 10–12 reps
⚠️ Cue: Long head makes up 60% of arm mass. Get a full 2-second deep stretch behind head.
Slot 6 • Triceps (Horseshoe Definition)

Pushdown / Lockout Pattern

3 sets × 12–15 reps
⚠️ Cue: Pin elbows to ribs like hinges. Squeeze triceps hard for 1 full second at bottom.

Day 2: Athletic Lower A (5 Dedicated Exercises)

Quad hypertrophy, ankle/knee stability, and deep core strengthening for Anterior Pelvic Tilt (APT) reversal.

Slot 1 • Quad Primary Compound

Squat Pattern

3 sets × 6–8 reps
⚠️ Cue: Feet 4 inches forward on Smith keeps torso vertical—eliminates lower back APT strain!
Slot 2 • Unilateral Quad & Glute

Single Leg Power (Zero Walking Space)

3 sets × 8–10 reps / leg
⚠️ Cue: Lean torso 20° forward over front thigh to activate glute-quad junction.
Slot 3 • Quad Isolation

Quad Burnout Pattern

3 sets × 10–12 reps
⚠️ Cue: Pause for 1 full second at peak contraction to trigger maximum metabolic stress.
Slot 4 • Calves (Ankle Stability & Sprint Power)

Calf Raise Pattern

4 sets × 12–15 reps
⚠️ Cue: Hold 2 full seconds in the bottom stretch; do not bounce out of Achilles tendon elasticity.
Slot 5 • Deep Core & Lower Abdominals

Pelvic Roll / Knee Raise (APT Fix)

3 sets × 12–15 reps
⚠️ Cue: Roll your pelvis upward toward your sternum at the top—reverses anterior pelvic tilt!

Day 3: Upper Pull (6 Dedicated Exercises)

V-Taper lats, thick upper back for desk posture, and biceps peaks. + Chicken Day!

Slot 1 • Latissimus Dorsi (V-Taper Width)

Vertical Pull Pattern (FRONT TO UPPER CHEST ONLY)

3 sets × 8–10 reps
⚠️ Cue: 🚨 FRONT ONLY! Lean back 10–15°. Drive elbows down to back pockets. Never pull behind neck!
Slot 2 • Upper Back Thickness & Rhomboids

Horizontal Row Pattern

3 sets × 8–10 reps
⚠️ Cue: Chest flat on incline pad takes 100% strain off lower back. Pull elbows past ribcage.
Slot 3 • Rear Delts & Scapular Retraction

Rear Shoulder Realignment

4 sets × 12–15 reps
⚠️ Cue: Rotate thumbs backward at the finish to recruit the rotator cuff and reverse IT desk posture.
Slot 4 • Lat Stretch Isolation

Straight-Arm Pulldown / Pullover

3 sets × 12–15 reps
⚠️ Cue: Slight torso lean, soft elbows. Drive hands down to thighs purely using your lats.
Slot 5 • Biceps Brachialis & Forearms

Hammer Curl Pattern (Thick Arms)

3 sets × 10–12 reps
⚠️ Cue: Neutral palms grip builds the brachialis beneath the bicep, pushing the arm wider.
Slot 6 • Biceps Peak Hypertrophy

Bicep Long Head Stretch

3 sets × 10–12 reps
⚠️ Cue: Keep arms behind torso on incline bench to place long head under extreme stretch.

Day 4: Athletic Lower B (5 Dedicated Exercises)

Posterior chain, hamstring elasticity, and sprinting stability. 48-hour buffer before Saturday cricket!

Slot 1 • Hamstrings & Glutes (Hip Hinge)

Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

3 sets × 8–10 reps
⚠️ Cue: Soft knees. Push hips back toward the wall behind you. Shave weights down shins.
Slot 2 • Glute Max (APT Reversal)

Hip Extension Pattern (Zero Floor / Awkwardness)

3 sets × 10–12 reps
⚠️ Cue: Flare toes out 30°, round upper back slightly to isolate glutes, lock hips in full extension at top.
Slot 3 • Hamstrings (Knee Flexion)

Leg Curl Pattern (Sprint Protection)

3 sets × 10–12 reps
⚠️ Cue: Crucial for sprint resilience: protects hamstrings from tearing during Saturday turf cricket.
Slot 4 • Core & Cricket Performance

Cricket Core / Sprint Endurance Pattern

3 sets × 12–15 reps
⚠️ Cue: Pick the movement that matches your goal: heavy six-pack abs, calf sprint endurance, or rotational cricket batting power.
Slot 5 • Grip Strength & Sprint Core

Loaded Carry / Grip Hold

3 sets × 45–60s hold
⚠️ Cue: Stand tall with chest puffed out and shoulders packed back. Iron forearms and core brace.

Day 5: Upper Proportions (6 Dedicated Exercises)

Golden Ratio focus: 3D deltoid caps, arm circumference, vascular forearms, and clavicular chest density.

Slot 1 • Clavicular Density Touch-Up

Incline Chest Density

3 sets × 8–10 reps
⚠️ Cue: Second weekly stimulus for the upper chest shelf to fill out shirts with zero shoulder strain.
Slot 2 • Lateral Delts (V-Taper Width)

Giant Shoulder Width

4 sets × 12–15 reps
⚠️ Cue: Lead with elbows. Maintain a slight 15° forward torso lean to keep traps turned off.
Slot 3 • 3D Rear Delts

Rear Shoulder Definition

3 sets × 12–15 reps
⚠️ Cue: Do not squeeze scapulae together; think of pushing hands wide outward in an expanding circle.
Slot 4 • Triceps (Lateral Horseshoe)

Triceps Isolation

3 sets × 10–12 reps
⚠️ Cue: Isolate the lateral tricep head for a thick arm profile from the front and side.
Slot 5 • Biceps Fullness & Peak

Bicep Peak Hypertrophy

3 sets × 10–12 reps
⚠️ Cue: Supinate wrists hard at the top (turn pinky finger outward) to squeeze the peak.
Slot 6 • Vascular Forearms & Grip

Forearm Wrist Curl Pattern

3 sets × 15–20 reps
⚠️ Cue: Forearms rested on bench. Let the bar roll down into fingers, then curl up with wrists.
