# Master Nutrition & Meal Distribution Blueprint

## 1. Daily Macronutrient Breakdown (Target: Muscular & Shredded)
- **Daily Calorie Target**: **2,250 – 2,350 kcal**
- **Protein Floor**: **145g – 155g / day** (~2.1 – 2.2g per kg body weight)
- **Healthy Fats**: **65g – 75g / day** (hormonal and testosterone support)
- **Complex Carbohydrates**: **~250g – 275g / day** (fuels heavy lifting & cricket sprint stamina)
- **Fiber**: **28g – 38g / day**
- **Hydration**: **3.5 – 4.0 Litres / day**
- **Creatine**: **3.1g daily** (3.0g Monohydrate + 0.1g MB CreAbsorb; morning or post-workout)
- **Omega-3**: **1 capsule daily** with lunch or dinner

---

## 2. Core Dietary Rules & 8 Default Daily Staples
1. **Whole Eggs Only (Up to 5/day)**: Never separate yolks. 3 with lunch, 2 with evening snack or dinner.
2. **Toned Milk (Up to 500ml/day)**: Split between morning oats and evening tea/coffee or pre-bed.
3. **Pottukadalai (Roasted Chana - 50g/day)**: Afternoon crunch providing ~11g protein and 5g fiber.
4. **Roasted Peanuts (15g) + Raw Cashews (15g)**: Essential micronutrients (zinc, magnesium) and healthy fats.
5. **Pure Honey (1–2 tsp / 15g)**: Natural sweetener for oats or fast carbs before gym.
6. **Rolled / Instant Oats (50g/day)**: Complex low-GI carbohydrates for sustained all-day energy.
7. **Soya Chunks Cap (45g dry-weighed raw)**: Provides ~23.4g protein on non-chicken days. Weighed dry on a digital scale before boiling.
8. **Chicken Routine (Wed / Sun - No Whey Needed)**:
   - Fresh morning batch of home chicken gravy.
   - Breakfast: Idli/Dosa with chicken gravy.
   - Lunch: Rice with chicken pieces + 2 whole boiled eggs + 150g curd.
   - Evening: Roasted chana (50g) + peanuts (15g) + 1 whole boiled egg.
   - Dinner: Chapati or Dosa with remaining chicken pieces.
9. **Lunch Lightness**: Lunch at ~01:45 PM before an afternoon nap. Kept moderate in glycemic load to prevent sluggishness.

## 3. Smart 1-to-1 Substitution Matrix (When Items are Missing)

| Unavailable Item | Smart 1-to-1 Replacement | Macros Maintained | Kitchen Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Soya Chunks (45g)** | **100g pan-seared Tofu + 1 Whole Boiled Egg** | ~21.5g Protein | Sauté tofu cubes with turmeric & salt. |
| **Both Soya & Tofu missing** | **150g Boiled Sundal (Chana) + 1 Boiled Egg** | ~20g Protein | Boiled black chana tempered with mustard seeds. |
| **Toned Milk (250ml)** | **200g Fresh Plain Curd (Dahi)** | ~8g Protein | Enjoy with lunch rice or whisk into buttermilk. |
| **Pottukadalai (50g)** | **30g Boiled Peanuts or 2 slices bread + 1 egg** | ~10g Protein | Quick toasted egg sandwich or salted peanuts. |
| **Cashews / Peanuts missing** | **10–12 Raw Almonds (Badam) or 15g Walnuts** | ~3g P, 8g Omega Fats | Soaked or raw with evening green tea/coffee. |
| **Pure Honey out** | **1 small ripe Banana (80g) or 15g Jaggery** | ~14g Carbs | Mash directly into warm oats. |
