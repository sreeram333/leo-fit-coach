## The 5-Day Master Exercise Blueprint (With Explicit Targeted Muscles)

### DAY 1: Upper Push (Upper Chest, 3D Shoulders, Triceps)
*Goal: Upper chest clavicular shelf, lateral delt width, and horseshoe triceps. (~65 mins)*

| # | Final Chosen Exercise | Explicit Targeted Muscle | Sets × Reps | Form & Stance Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seated Incline Chest Machine** | **Upper Chest (Clavicular Head Shelf)** | 3 × 8–10 | Handles align with upper chest. Press diagonally up and forward to fill the collarbone hollows. |
| 2 | **Flat Barbell Bench Press** | **Mid & Lower Chest (Sternal Pec Mass)** | 3 × 8–10 | Arch lower back slightly, heels pressed down. Lower bar to lower sternum for full chest thickness. |
| 3 | **Seated Dumbbell Lateral Raise** | **Side Shoulders (Lateral Deltoid Width)** | 4 × 12–15 | Torso 15° forward lean. Push knuckles out in the scapular plane (30° forward) to widen shoulder caps. |
| 4 | **Seated Barbell Overhead Press** | **Front Shoulders (Anterior Deltoid & Core)** | 3 × 8–10 | Barbell lowered to chin level, press straight overhead with tight core lockout. |
| 5 | **Seated Two-Arm Overhead DB Extension** | **Triceps (Long Head & Arm Thickness)** | 3 × 10–12 | Hold DB with diamond grip overhead. 2-second deep stretch behind neck (long head is 60% of arm). |
| 6 | **Cable Straight-Bar or Rope Pushdown** | **Triceps (Lateral Horseshoe Cut)** | 3 × 12–15 | Elbows pinned to ribs like door hinges. Push straight down and flare/squeeze for 1 full second at bottom. |

### DAY 2: Athletic Lower A (Quads, Calves, Deep Core & APT Reset)
*Goal: Quad sweep, knee stability, and strengthening deep core to flatten lower belly.*

| # | Final Chosen Exercise | Explicit Targeted Muscle | Sets × Reps | Form & Stance Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Smith Machine Squat (Feet 4" Forward)** | **Quads (Pure Knee Extension - Zero Back Shear)** | 3 × 6–8 | **Feet 4 inches forward** keeps spine vertical. 100% load on quads; completely eliminates APT lower back pain! |
| 2 | **Stationary Bulgarian Split Squat** | **Quads & Glutes (Unilateral Single-Leg Drive)** | 3 × 8–10 /leg | Rear foot on bench, torso tilted 20° forward. Drop front knee down to parallel for deep quad-glute tie-in. |
| 3 | **Leg Press (Narrow & Low Foot Placement)** | **Quads (Vastus Lateralis Outer Sweep & Teardrop)** | 3 × 10–12 | **Feet placed LOW and NARROW (6" apart)** on sled. Maximum knee flexion targets the outer quad curve. |
| 4 | **Standing Calf Raise on Block with DB** | **Calves (Gastrocnemius - Upper Diamond & Spring)** | 4 × 12–15 | Ball of foot on block. 2-second dead pause at bottom deficit; builds spring for cricket sprint acceleration. |
| 5 | **Captain's Chair Straight Leg Raise** | **Deep Core & Lower Abs (Transverse Abdominis & APT Fix)** | 3 × 12–15 | Forearms on pads. Curl pelvis upward toward chest at the top—reverses anterior pelvic tilt! |

### DAY 3: Upper Pull (V-Taper Lats, Upper Back, Biceps) + *Chicken Day*
*Goal: Flare the lats, pull rounded shoulders back, and build bicep peaks.*

| # | Final Chosen Exercise | Explicit Targeted Muscle | Sets × Reps | Form & Stance Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Assisted Pull-Up Machine** | **Lats (Latissimus Dorsi V-Taper Width)** | 3 × 8–10 | **FRONT TO CHEST ONLY**. Pull chest to bar, drive elbows down into back pockets; never pull behind neck! |
| 2 | **Seated Cable Row (Neutral V-Bar Grip)** | **Mid-Back (Rhomboids, Lower Traps & Mid-Lats)** | 3 × 8–10 | **V-Bar attachment**. Pull handle into navel. Torso stays still; retract and squeeze shoulder blades back. |
| 3 | **Reverse Pec Deck Machine (Rear Fly)** | **Rear Shoulders (Posterior Delts & Posture)** | 4 × 12–15 | Chest against pad. Push handles outward in a wide expanding circle to build 3D rear delts and fix desk slouch. |
| 4 | **Dumbbell Pullover across Flat Bench** | **Lats & Serratus (Lat Stretch & Ribcage Expansion)** | 3 × 12–15 | Perpendicular on bench. Lower dumbbell in an arc over head; feel massive lat stretch and serratus expansion. |
| 5 | **Seated Dumbbell Hammer Curl** | **Arm Thickness (Brachialis & Outer Forearms)** | 3 × 10–12 | Palms facing each other. Builds the brachialis muscle beneath the bicep, pushing arm width outward. |
| 6 | **Preacher Curl (DB or Barbell)** | **Biceps Peak (Biceps Brachii Short/Long Head)** | 3 × 10–12 | Upper triceps locked on pad. Stop 1" before full lockout to protect tendon, curl dumbbell/bar to chin. |

### DAY 4: Athletic Lower B (Hamstrings, Glute Drive & Cricket Sprint Recovery)
*Goal: Posterior chain elasticity, sprinting power, and 48-hour recovery buffer before Saturday cricket.*

| # | Final Chosen Exercise | Explicit Targeted Muscle | Sets × Reps | Form & Stance Focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Barbell Romanian Deadlift (RDL)** | **Hamstrings & Glute-Ham Tie-In (Hip Hinge)** | 3 × 8–10 | Soft knees. Push hips back toward wall. Shave bar down shins until deep hamstring stretch; protects sprinting hamstrings. |
| 2 | **High & Wide Stance Leg Press** | **Gluteus Maximus (Glute Power & APT Reversal)** | 3 × 10–12 | **Feet placed at VERY TOP EDGE of plate, WIDE stance, toes 20° out**. Drive through heels! Zero lower back strain. |
| 3 | **Lying Hamstring Leg Curl Machine** | **Hamstrings (Knee Flexion & Sprint Resilience)** | 3 × 10–12 | Hips pinned flat to pad. Curl pad directly toward glutes. Strengthens hamstrings against sudden sprint tears! |
| 4 | **Seated Calf Raise (Weights on Knees)** | **Calves (Soleus Muscle - Anti-Cramp Sprint Engine)** | 3 × 12–15 | Sit on bench, balls of feet on plate, dumbbells/bar on knees. Targets soleus to absorb sprint impact & prevent cramps. |
| 5 | **Barbell Over-Bench Plate Pinch / Hold** | **Forearms & Grip (Wrist Flexors & Isometric Hold)** | 3 × 45–60s hold | Stand tall or hold heavy plates/barbell; builds iron forearm grip and traps for bowling/fielding. |

## Double Progression Rules (How to Progress Every Single Week)
- Every movement has a target rep bracket (e.g. 8–10 reps or 12–15 reps).
- **If you hit the top number on all sets** (e.g., 10, 10, 10 reps): Bump the weight by $+1.25\text{ kg}$ to $+2.5\text{ kg}$ next session.
- **If you hit lower reps** (e.g., 9, 8, 7 reps): Stay at that exact weight next session and fight for $+1\text{ rep}$.
