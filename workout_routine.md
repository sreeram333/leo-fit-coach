## Daily 5-Minute Morning Posture & APT Protocol (Every Morning post-washroom)
1. **Stomach Vacuums**: 4 sets × 20 seconds hold. Exhale all air, pull belly button back toward spine. Strengthens transverse abdominis, flattens lower belly protrusion.  
   🎥 **Video Tutorial**: [How To Do A Stomach Vacuum (Mind Pump TV)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5GsE6ZHn_E)
2. **Kneeling Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch with Posterior Pelvic Tilt**: 2 sets × 45–60s per side. Squeeze rear glute to tuck pelvis under, shift forward slightly. Releases tight psoas from WFH IT sitting.  
   🎥 **Video Tutorial**: [Bulletproof Guide to the Half Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch (The Prehab Guys)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gqoPYLUgP48)
3. **Glute Squeezes & Posterior Pelvic Tilt Tucks**: 2 sets × 10 reps (3s squeeze at top). Wakes up glutes before sitting at desk and resets pelvic tilt.  
   🎥 **Video Tutorial**: [How To Fix Lower Back Pelvic Tilt Posture (Athlean-X / Tone & Tighten)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mkbNJKiWNlI)

## Double Progression Rules (How to Progress Every Single Week)
- Every movement has a target rep bracket (e.g. 8–10 reps or 12–15 reps).
- **If you hit the top number on all sets** (e.g., 10, 10, 10 reps): Bump the weight by $+1.25\text{ kg}$ to $+2.5\text{ kg}$ next session.
- **If you hit lower reps** (e.g., 9, 8, 7 reps): Stay at that exact weight next session and fight for $+1\text{ rep}$.
