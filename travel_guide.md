# Travel Protocol: 16-Day Maintenance & Damage Control (Nov 2 – Nov 17)

## Mission Objective: "Preserve & Maintain"
During these 16 days away from home with no gym access, zero equipment, and difficult vegetarian dining:
* **The Goal is NOT aggressive fat loss or muscle building**: The goal is **100% Muscle Preservation and Zero Fat Regain**.
* When you return on Nov 18, you should step on the scale at the exact same weight (~71 kg) with your strength primed to jump straight back into the gym.

---

## 1. Nutrition Survival Rules on the Road
When high-protein home cooking and Whey are unavailable:

1. **Realistic Protein Floor (70g – 90g/day)**:
   - Do not stress about hitting 145g protein. 70g–90g is more than enough to prevent muscle catabolism over a 2-week period when combined with bodyweight maintenance stimulus.
   - **Order Smart at Restaurants**:
     - *Paneer dishes* (Paneer Bhurji / Kadai Paneer / Tandoori Paneer Tikka — ask for less oil/butter).
     - *Curd / Plain Buttermilk (Chaas)*: Have 1-2 glasses of plain salted chaas with every lunch/dinner (aids digestion and provides light protein).
     - *Dal Tadka / Chana Masala / Rajma*: Solid whole food staples.
     - *Milk*: 1 glass warm milk at night or morning.
2. **Packable Pocket Snacks (Crucial)**:
   - Pack 2 ziplock pouches in your luggage before leaving:
     - 500g Roasted Chana (Pottukadalai)
     - 250g Roasted Peanuts
   - Munching 30g–40g of roasted chana in the afternoon gives you 8g–10g pure protein without relying on restaurants.
3. **The "Single-Carb" Plate Rule**:
   - At buffets or meals, do not eat both large mounds of rice AND multiple naans/rotis. Pick one: either 2 Rotis/Phulkas OR 1 cup Rice.
   - Fill half your plate with salad/cucumbers and dal first.
4. **Hydration**: Drink 3+ litres of bottled water. Travel bloating is almost always water retention from restaurant sodium + sitting, not fat gain.

## 2. The 15-Minute Zero-Equipment Hotel Room Maintainer
*Frequency: Once every 2 days (e.g., 3 to 4 times a week). Takes only 15 minutes before your morning shower.*

| # | Movement Target | Exercise | Technique Cue | Sets × Reps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Upper Body Push & Chest** | Feet-Elevated Push-Ups (or Regular Push-Ups) | Feet on hotel bed/chair, slow 3-second descent | 3 × 12–15 |
| 2 | **Legs & Quads (Unilateral)** | Bulgarian Split Squats (Single Leg) | Back foot resting on hotel bed or chair | 3 × 10–12 / leg |
| 3 | **Upper Back & Posture** | Prone Y-T-W Raises (Lying face-down on bed/carpet) | Squeeze shoulder blades hard at top for 2 seconds | 3 × 12 reps |
| 4 | **Glutes & Hamstrings (APT Fix)** | Single-Leg Glute Bridge (on floor) | Drive through heel, 2-second glute squeeze at top | 3 × 12 / leg |
| 5 | **Core & Abdomen** | Stomach Vacuums + Deadbugs | 4 × 20s Vacuums + 15 Deadbugs per side | 3 sets |

---

## 3. Daily Step Target
* Keep moving: Target **8,000 – 10,000 steps/day** through walking and daily travel activity. This keeps your NEAT (Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis) high and handles any extra restaurant calories effortlessly.
